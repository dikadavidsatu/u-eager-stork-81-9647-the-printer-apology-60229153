The Printer Apology: one line of at most 120 characters, an apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it. The printer's defence is timing: it only ever jams on a Friday at 4:58, after a week of cheap paper and tray-slaps, because one of us had to learn work-life balance. The program validates the line against the gate (1 to 120 characters, a single line, not only whitespace, plain printable ASCII, an apology's opening and a finished note's full stop), writes it to entry.txt, then re-reads the file and verifies it byte for byte.
